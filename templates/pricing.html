Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?

We accept WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF, OGG, and M4A files up to 500MB.

How long does mastering take?

Most tracks are mastered within 2-5 minutes. Longer tracks may take a bit more time.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes! Every new account gets 1 free master to try our service.

What's the difference between presets?

Each preset optimizes for different sounds - Warm adds richness, Bright adds clarity, Punchy adds impact.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied with your master, contact us and we'll make it right.

Is my music safe?

Absolutely. We never share or use your music. Files are automatically deleted after 30 days.
