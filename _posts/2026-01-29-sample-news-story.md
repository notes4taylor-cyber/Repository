## How to Write Stories

Stories are written in **Markdown**, a simple formatting language. Here are some basics:

- Use `**bold**` for **bold text**
- Use `*italics*` for *italics*
- Use `## Heading` for section headings
- Use `-` for bullet points

## Adding Images

To add an image to your story, first place the image in the `/assets/images/` folder, then reference it like this:

```markdown
![Description of image](/assets/images/your-image.jpg)
```
